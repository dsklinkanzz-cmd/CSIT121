Lab 05 - UI/UX Evaluation

Maryland Health Connection

What is the URL of the website?

https://www.marylandhealthconnection.gov/

What is the name of the website?

Maryland Health Connection

Who is the site's target audience?

The site's target audience are Maryland residents who are looking for health insurance options and information about the state's health insurance marketplace.

How is the site organized?

The site has a hierarchical structure, it is organized into several sections, including a homepage, information about health insurance plans, a plan comparison tool, and resources for consumers.

Which CRAP design principles does the site use? Provide at least one example.

The site uses the principle of C "Contrast" by using a color scheme that makes text easy to read against the background.

What is the Audit Score according to the Accessibility Checker?

The website received an accessibility score of 92 out of 100.

What is the site's effectiveness? Does it help users complete actions accurately?

The site is effective in helping users find information about health insurance plans and compare different options. It has clear navigation menus and search functionality.

What is the site's efficiency? Can users perform tasks quickly?

Some processes of the website may be time-consuming, especially for users who are not familiar with the interface. This might not be related to the design itself.

How is the engagement? Is it pleasant to use and appropiate for its industry/topic?

The site is engaging and provides a pleasant user experience with clear visuals and helpful information. It is appropriate for its industry, focusing on health insurance and related resources.

Make at least one recommendation to improve this website based on what you learned in this module.

One recommendation would be to implement a form that automatically advises users based on their input.
